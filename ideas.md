# Creta Car — Visual Direction

## Reference Ground Truth

The provided Mercedes-AMG reference is treated as the visual benchmark: cinematic automotive storytelling, dramatic full-bleed vehicle imagery, high-contrast dark surfaces, oversized condensed typography, purposeful motion, and a premium case-study rhythm. The Creta Car site will be an original interpretation with its own palette, copy, mark, and layout logic.

## Three Initial Directions

### Theme Name: Dark Editorial Performance
Very Brief Intro: A cinematic, gallery-like automotive experience built around graphite, acid-lime signal color, and oversized display type. It feels like a launch film translated into a website.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Monsoon Utility / Future Terrain
Very Brief Intro: A tactile, weather-aware direction pairing mineral neutrals with wet-road blue and topographic linework. It frames Creta as a confident companion for everyday escapes.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Quiet Precision
Very Brief Intro: A restrained ivory-and-ink product journal that uses elegant typography, generous negative space, and technical annotations to make the car feel considered and exact.

## Chosen Direction: Dark Editorial Performance

### Design Movement
Contemporary automotive art direction fused with Swiss International Typographic Style and motion-design title cards: strict alignment, oversized numerals, cropped typography, and a controlled tension between raw material and editorial restraint.

### Core Principles
1. Treat every section like a frame in a launch film, with one dominant idea and one clear visual action.
2. Use asymmetry and offset columns to create forward motion instead of centered marketing layouts.
3. Pair hard, technical surfaces with soft atmospheric image treatment so the vehicle feels both engineered and lived-in.
4. Make data feel collectible: performance figures become oversized graphic objects, not small feature bullets.

### Color Philosophy
Graphite-black and smoked silver create the visual equivalent of a sculpted body panel and give the vehicle space to command attention. Acid-lime is reserved for interaction states, route markers, and the most important data points; it should feel like an instrument light, not decoration. Warm off-white keeps long copy readable and adds a print-editorial counterweight to the dark field.

### Layout Paradigm
A vertical narrative with a fixed left rail for chapter markers and a wide right canvas for image-led moments. Hero copy is anchored low and offset from the vehicle; specs interrupt the flow as oversized typographic slabs; the final CTA is a quiet, high-contrast landing rather than a loud sales block.

### Signature Elements
- A thin lime route line that travels through chapter labels and expands into a circular control on hover.
- Oversized ghost numerals behind technical stat blocks.
- Fine-grain film texture and hairline rules that make the page feel like a premium printed lookbook in motion.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like changing focus on a camera rig: decisive, smooth, and brief. Buttons shift by a few pixels, image crops tighten on hover, and the chapter rail highlights the user’s position without stealing attention. Every interactive element has a clear purpose and visible focus state.

### Animation
Use 180–280ms ease-out transitions for hover, menu, and button states. Reveal major sections with slight vertical translation and opacity only, staggered by 50–70ms. The hero image should have a slow, almost imperceptible scale drift while the text stays crisp. Respect reduced-motion preferences by disabling non-essential transforms and auto-play effects.

### Typography System
Display: Space Grotesk, 700–800, tightly tracked for hero statements and section titles. Body: Manrope, 400–600, with generous line height for product narrative. Micro labels: IBM Plex Mono, 500, uppercase, letter spacing 0.14em. Use a 12-column desktop rhythm with display type spanning 5–7 columns and supporting copy spanning 3–4.

### Brand Essence
Creta Car is the confident compact SUV for drivers who want everyday capability with a sharper point of view — distinct, assured, kinetic.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are short, active, and slightly provocative. CTAs sound like an invitation into the machine, not a generic conversion prompt. Microcopy is precise and calm.

Example lines:
- “Make the everyday feel engineered.”
- “Take the long way home.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact symbol built from two offset chevrons forming a forward-facing C, paired with a custom uppercase CRETA wordmark set with a cut in the crossbar of the E. The symbol should work independently as a favicon and as the chapter rail marker.

### Signature Brand Color
Signal Lime — #C8F24A. Used sparingly, it is ownable, legible on graphite, and carries the feeling of instrument illumination and forward movement.
